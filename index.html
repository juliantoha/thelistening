The bar is simple. You play.

You spent years learning an instrument. Then work happened. The Listening is where the instrument comes back out. Not for an audience. For a room.

Each evening has two movements. The Set is the music: short performances, prepared or nearly so, in a living room with the lights low. Chamber pieces, jazz standards, the thing you have been picking at for months. Perfect is not the standard. Played is the standard.

Then the Afterwards. Wine, or not. The conversations that only happen among people who just heard each other play. The Set is the music. The Afterwards is the reason.
